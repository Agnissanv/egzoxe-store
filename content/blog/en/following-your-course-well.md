**1. Follow the directions.** Take the amount shown on the product page and packaging. Do not add more thinking it will work faster.

**2. Pick a fixed time.** At breakfast or dinner, depending on what is recommended. A routine helps you not forget.

**3. Stick to the recommended duration.** A course is followed for the stated length. If in doubt, ask before you start.

Store your product in a dry place, away from heat and light, and out of reach of children.
