A food supplement complements your diet. It provides nutrients or plants that can support your daily balance.

But it does not replace a varied meal, sleep or physical activity. And it never replaces medical treatment. If you have a lasting symptom, see a health professional.

At EGZOXÉ store, we prefer to explain clearly what a product contains rather than promise miracles.
